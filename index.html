♪ Chiptune Designer_

Click or drag to paint notes. Right-drag erases. Hold Shift while dragging to keep the same pitch. Click a piano key to hear it. Highlighted rows are in the key; the green edge marks the root. 🎲 generates a whole tune, or re-rolls one part in the current key. Each voice plays one note per step. Enable Tie repeats to turn runs of the same note into one long note. Space plays/stops, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes. Your pattern autosaves in this browser.
